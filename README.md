# Instant Free Tools

Free browser tools from [instantfreetools.com](https://instantfreetools.com/). No sign-up, and what you type stays in your browser.

This repository is just the list. Each link goes to the live tool.

## Date tools

- [Date calculator](https://instantfreetools.com/date-calculator/): Add or subtract days, count days between dates, or count business days, all on one page.
- [Age calculator](https://instantfreetools.com/age-calculator/): Exact age in years, months and days, plus a countdown to the next birthday.
- [Days between dates](https://instantfreetools.com/days-between-dates/): Days, weeks, months and weekdays between two dates.
- [Business days calculator](https://instantfreetools.com/business-days-calculator/): Working days between two dates, or a date N business days away. Holidays for 200+ countries.
- [How many days until](https://instantfreetools.com/days-until/): Countdown to Christmas, Thanksgiving or any date you pick.
- [Week number](https://instantfreetools.com/week-number/): What week is it? ISO and US week numbers for any date, with every week of the year.

## Time and clocks

- [Hours calculator](https://instantfreetools.com/hours-calculator/): Hours between two times, minus breaks, for a shift or a whole week. Decimal hours and pay.
- [World clock](https://instantfreetools.com/world-clock/): The current time in cities around the world and the time difference from you.
- [Countdown timer](https://instantfreetools.com/countdown-timer/): Online timer with alarm, presets from 1 to 60 minutes and full screen.
- [Time zone converter](https://instantfreetools.com/time-zone-converter/): Convert a time between cities, with daylight saving handled for you.
- [Online stopwatch](https://instantfreetools.com/stopwatch/): Stopwatch with laps, fastest and slowest lap, and Space to start or stop.
- [Alarm clock](https://instantfreetools.com/alarm-clock/): Set alarms with a label and sound, snooze or stop, and a big live clock.

## Math and grades

- [Percentage calculator](https://instantfreetools.com/percentage-calculator/): Percent of a number, percent change, and what percent one number is of another.
- [Grade calculator](https://instantfreetools.com/grade-calculator/): Weighted class grade as a percent and letter, and the score you need on the final.
- [GPA calculator](https://instantfreetools.com/gpa-calculator/): Unweighted and weighted GPA on a 4.0 scale, with Honors and AP classes and cumulative GPA.
- [Average calculator](https://instantfreetools.com/average-calculator/): Mean, median, mode, sum, range and standard deviation of a list, plus weighted averages.
- [Ratio calculator](https://instantfreetools.com/ratio-calculator/): Simplify a ratio, find the missing number in a proportion, or split an amount by a ratio.
- [Fraction to decimal](https://instantfreetools.com/fraction-to-decimal/): Fractions, mixed numbers and decimals both ways, with repeating decimals.

## Money and health

- [BMI calculator](https://instantfreetools.com/bmi-calculator/): Body mass index from height and weight in kg or lb, with the adult category and healthy weight range.
- [Loan calculator](https://instantfreetools.com/loan-calculator/): Monthly payment, total interest and the full payment schedule, with extra payments.
- [Compound interest calculator](https://instantfreetools.com/compound-interest-calculator/): How savings grow with compound interest and monthly deposits, year by year.
- [Tip calculator](https://instantfreetools.com/tip-calculator/): Work out the tip and split the bill, with rounding and tip before tax.

## Words and text

- [Typing test](https://instantfreetools.com/typing-test/): Check your typing speed in words per minute and your accuracy, in 30 seconds to 3 minutes.
- [Word and character counter](https://instantfreetools.com/character-counter/): Count words, characters and sentences, with reading time and limits for X, SMS and more.
- [Word unscrambler](https://instantfreetools.com/word-unscrambler/): Find every word you can make from your letters, with blanks and start or end letters.
- [Case converter](https://instantfreetools.com/case-converter/): UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case, camelCase, snake_case and more.
- [Lorem ipsum generator](https://instantfreetools.com/lorem-ipsum-generator/): Placeholder text by paragraphs, sentences or words, as plain text or HTML.
- [Diff checker (compare text)](https://instantfreetools.com/compare-text/): See what was added, removed or changed between two versions of a text.

## Random and games

- [Spin the wheel](https://instantfreetools.com/spin-the-wheel/): Spin a wheel of names for a fair pick, or draw several winners, make teams or shuffle the order.
- [Random number generator](https://instantfreetools.com/random-number-generator/): Pick one or many numbers in any range, with or without repeats.
- [Password generator](https://instantfreetools.com/password-generator/): Strong random passwords made on your device, with length and character options.
- [Flip a coin](https://instantfreetools.com/coin-flip/): Heads or tails in one tap, or up to 100 coins with a running count.
- [Dice roller](https://instantfreetools.com/dice-roller/): Roll d4 to d100, up to 20 dice, with the total. Type 2d6+3 too.
- [Random name generator](https://instantfreetools.com/random-name-generator/): Make up realistic first, last or full names, up to 100 at once.
- [Random word generator](https://instantfreetools.com/random-word-generator/): Random everyday nouns, verbs and adjectives, or any dictionary word, by letter and length.

## Codes and converters

- [QR code generator](https://instantfreetools.com/qr-code-generator/): QR codes for links, Wi-Fi, text, email, phone and SMS. Download PNG or SVG.
- [Morse code translator](https://instantfreetools.com/morse-code-translator/): Text to Morse code and back, with sound and flashing light at any speed.
- [Unit converter](https://instantfreetools.com/unit-converter/): Length, weight, temperature, volume, area, speed and time, metric and US or UK.
- [Binary translator](https://instantfreetools.com/binary-translator/): Text to binary code and back, with hex and decimal. Works with accents and emoji.
